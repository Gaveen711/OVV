Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 July 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Ocean View Villas ("we", "us", "our") collects, uses, and protects the personal information you share with us through this website, including the Register Your Interest form.

Information we collect

When you register your interest or contact us, we may collect:

  • Your first and last name
  • Your email address and phone number
  • Your country of residence
  • How you heard about us
  • Any message or information you choose to share with us

We also collect limited technical information automatically, such as the page from which a form was submitted, to help us respond to your enquiry accurately.

How we use your information

  • To respond to your enquiry and arrange private presentations of the residences
  • To keep you informed about Ocean View Villas, where you have opted in to receive updates
  • To maintain records of our communications with you

How your data is stored

Enquiries submitted through this website are processed and stored in HubSpot, the customer relationship management platform we use to manage communications. HubSpot processes this data on our behalf in accordance with its own privacy and security commitments. You can read more in HubSpot's Privacy Policy.

Sharing your information

We do not sell your personal information. Your details are shared only with the Ocean View Villas sales team and the service providers who help us operate this website and manage enquiries, and only to the extent needed to respond to you.

How long we keep your information

We keep your enquiry details for as long as needed to handle your enquiry and any ongoing relationship with you. If you ask us to remove your information, we will delete it unless we are required to keep it for legal reasons.

Your rights

You can at any time:

  • Ask for a copy of the personal information we hold about you
  • Ask us to correct or delete your information
  • Withdraw consent to receive updates from us

To exercise any of these rights, contact us using the details below and we will respond as soon as reasonably possible.
